# POL-200 Delivery Performance and Late Shipments

*Fictional policy for a portfolio demo.*

## 1. On-time delivery
A delivery is on time when the goods are received on or before the expected delivery date on the PO.

## 2. Grace period
Deliveries received up to 3 business days after the expected date are treated as on time and are not escalated.

## 3. Late deliveries
A delivery received more than 3 business days late is a late delivery. The buyer must record the delay in the exception report and notify the supplier relationship manager.

## 4. Late delivery penalties
Where the supplier agreement includes a delay clause, a penalty of 0.5% of the PO value per business day late applies, capped at 5% of the PO value. Penalties are deducted from the next payment only after approval by the procurement manager.

## 5. Repeated lateness
Three late deliveries by the same supplier within any 90-day period trigger a supplier performance review.
