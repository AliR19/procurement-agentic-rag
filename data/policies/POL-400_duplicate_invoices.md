# POL-400 Duplicate Invoice Handling

*Fictional policy for a portfolio demo.*

## 1. Definition
A duplicate invoice is a second invoice from the same supplier for the same PO with matching line items and total, even if the invoice number or date differs.

## 2. Detection
Accounts payable checks for more than one invoice against a single PO before releasing payment.

## 3. Handling
When a duplicate is suspected, payment of the later invoice is blocked and an exception report is required. The supplier is asked to confirm in writing whether the second invoice is a correction or a mistake. Never pay both invoices.

## 4. Recovery
If a duplicate payment was already made, accounts payable requests a credit note or refund from the supplier within 10 business days.
