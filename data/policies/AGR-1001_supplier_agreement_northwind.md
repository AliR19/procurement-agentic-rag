# AGR-1001 Supplier Agreement: Northwind Industrial Supply (S1001)

*Fictional supplier agreement for a portfolio demo.*

## 1. Pricing
Prices are fixed per purchase order. A fuel surcharge of up to 1.5% may be added to freight lines only, and only with prior written notice.

## 2. Delivery
Standard lead time is 7 to 12 business days. The delay clause in POL-200 section 4 does not apply; late deliveries instead earn the buyer a 2% credit on the next invoice.

## 3. Payment terms
Net 30 from the invoice date.
