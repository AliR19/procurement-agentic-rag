# AGR-1008 Supplier Agreement: Tidewater Logistics Parts (S1008)

*Fictional supplier agreement for a portfolio demo.*

## 1. Pricing
Unit prices are fixed for the duration of each purchase order. Price changes apply only to future purchase orders and need 30 days written notice.

## 2. Delivery
Standard lead time is 5 to 15 business days. The delay clause in POL-200 section 4 applies to this agreement.

## 3. Invoicing
Invoices must quote the PO number and may only bill for goods actually shipped. Partial shipments must be invoiced separately and marked as partial.

## 4. Payment terms
Net 30 from the invoice date.
