# POL-500 Supplier Onboarding and Payment Terms

*Fictional policy for a portfolio demo.*

## 1. Onboarding
New suppliers must complete a vendor questionnaire, provide a tax registration number and be approved by the procurement manager before any PO is issued.

## 2. Standard payment terms
The standard payment term is Net 30 from the invoice date. Suppliers may be granted Net 45 or Net 60 by the procurement manager when it is written into the supplier agreement.

## 3. Early payment discounts
Where the supplier agreement offers an early payment discount, it applies only when the invoice passed three-way matching and was approved within the discount window.

## 4. Supplier ratings
Suppliers are rated from 1 to 5 each quarter on delivery performance, invoice accuracy and responsiveness. A rating below 3.5 requires a performance improvement plan.
