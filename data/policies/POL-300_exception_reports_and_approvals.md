# POL-300 Exception Reports and Approval Authority

*Fictional policy for a portfolio demo.*

## 1. When an exception report is required
An exception report is required when an invoice is blocked by three-way matching (POL-100), when a delivery is late beyond the grace period (POL-200), or when a duplicate invoice is suspected (POL-400).

## 2. Contents
An exception report must include the PO number, the invoice number, a short summary, the list of discrepancies with amounts, and the action requested.

## 3. Approval authority
The financial impact of the discrepancy decides who approves the resolution:
- Under CAD 500: accounts payable supervisor.
- CAD 500 to CAD 4,999.99: procurement manager.
- CAD 5,000 or more: procurement director.

## 4. Human approval before filing
Exception reports are filed in the ERP only after a human has reviewed the discrepancies. Automated tools may draft a report but must not submit one without explicit human approval.

## 5. Records
Filed reports are retained for 7 years.
